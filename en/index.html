FAQ

Common questions before we start

Didn't find your answer? Ask the assistant or message me directly.

Ask me on WhatsApp
Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. The Starter plan is built for new and small businesses: we start with a plan and budget that fit your stage, then scale gradually based on results.

Do you only manage campaigns?

Campaign management is the core, but the work also covers strategy, tracking setup (Pixel and Conversions API), ad concepts and copy, and shoot direction. Design and editing are included in Scale or available as an add-on. If you need a website, an app or protection for your accounts, website and app development and cybersecurity services are also available.

Is the ad budget included?

No. The plan fee covers management and optimization; the ad budget is paid directly to the platforms (Meta, TikTok or Snapchat) from your account, so spending stays transparent and under your control.

How long until a campaign shows results?

The first two weeks are testing and the algorithm's learning phase; results usually start stabilizing and improving from week three. It depends on the product, budget and creative quality.

Do you provide reports?

Yes. Depending on your plan you get a monthly or weekly report in plain language: what you spent, what it achieved, and the next step. Scale includes a live reporting dashboard.

Do you work with real estate?

Yes. Real estate is one of the sectors I work in, with a focus on qualified leads instead of random inquiries, through precise targeting, qualifying forms and clear tracking.

Can I start with a small budget?

Yes, you can start small to test. I recommend at least $300/month so there's enough data to optimize, and we'll set the right number together after the consultation.

Does the ad account stay in my name?

Yes. I work on your ad account and pages through Business Manager access, and all data always stays yours.

What does the ad account audit include?

I review your ad accounts, pixel and tracking, and campaign structure, then deliver a report of the biggest mistakes and a clear improvement plan, plus a WhatsApp walkthrough. You can apply it yourself or we can work on it together.

What are UGC ads, and do they fit my business?

UGC ads are videos in a real customer's style, like a try-on, an unboxing or an honest review. They suit most stores, brands and restaurants because they feel native in the feed and build more trust; we'll decide in the consultation if they fit your product.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month-to-month, and you can stop anytime with 15 days' notice.
