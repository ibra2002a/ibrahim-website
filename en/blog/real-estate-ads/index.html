Real estate ads: how to get serious buyers instead of random inquiries

November 14, 20262 min read

Anyone who advertises property knows the pattern: lots of messages asking "how much?" and then silence. The usual cause is an ad built to collect as many messages as possible, not the best buyers. The fix is to build the campaign so it filters people before they reach you.

1. Put the key details in the ad itself

Location, property type, size, and the price range or payment terms. This may seem like it reduces messages, and that's exactly the point: people who message you after seeing these details are closer to a decision.

2. Use a form that asks the right questions

In Meta lead forms you can choose the Higher intent form type instead of the volume-focused one, and add short questions such as:

  • What is your approximate budget?
  • When are you planning to buy or move?
  • Cash or installments?

Each question lowers the number of leads a little, but raises their quality a lot.

3. Video sells the property before the viewing

A real video tour of the property, with shots of the area and nearby services, helps people decide before they call. You can later retarget people who watched most of the video, because they showed real interest.

4. Fast follow-up is part of the campaign

Someone who fills in your form today may contact three other agencies in the same hour. A quick call or WhatsApp message right after the lead arrives makes a big difference to the number of viewings booked. The best campaign can still lose to a slow reply.

5. Measure what actually matters

Instead of stopping at cost per message, track:

  • Cost per qualified lead (one whose answers fit what you offer).
  • Number of viewings booked.
  • Number of deals that started from the ad.

These are the numbers that tell you which campaign deserves more budget.
