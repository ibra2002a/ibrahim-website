UGC Ads

People trust someone like them more than a polished ad. UGC ads show your product through a real customer's eyes, so they feel native in the feed and keep viewers watching and considering a purchase.

Book your free consultation Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • UGC concepts and scripts written to sell
  • Creator and shoot direction
  • Platform-native editing with on-screen text
  • Testing multiple hooks on the same video
  • Using videos across TikTok, Meta and Snapchat ads
  • Refreshing content before the audience tires of it

Who is it for?

  • Beauty, skincare and fashion products
  • Online stores and new products
  • Restaurants and food products
  • Any brand wanting more trust than ads alone

How we work

  1. 01Understand your product, buying reasons and objections
  2. 02Write scripts with multiple angles and hooks
  3. 03Film with a creator or guide you to film yourself
  4. 04Test videos in ads and repeat what wins

Questions about this service

What's the difference between UGC and a regular ad?

UGC looks like a normal person's experience, not a company ad, so it often gets longer watch time and more trust, especially on TikTok and Reels.

Who films the videos?

Depends on your plan: a script you film yourself, me directing a creator, or several videos monthly with creators.

How many videos do I need?

Start with several versions to test, since you can't know the winner in advance. We set the number by budget.
