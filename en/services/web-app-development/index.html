Questions about this service

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on the size of the project and the pages and features you need. We set the price after understanding your needs in the consultation, and it's written and clear before we start.

How long does it take?

A landing page or business website takes less time than a store or an app. We agree on a clear timeline before starting, based on the project's size.

Will the website be in my name?

Yes. The domain, hosting and files are in your name and fully yours.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes. We agree on a support period after launch, and we can continue with updates and new features as you need.
