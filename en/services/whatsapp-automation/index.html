WhatsApp Ads & Auto-Replies

In Iraq most sales happen in messages, and the problem isn't getting messages but answering them fast. I connect your ads straight to WhatsApp and set up auto-replies that answer common questions and collect customer details before they reach you.

Book a consultation Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Instagram and Facebook
  • Automatic welcome message and quick questions
  • Ready replies for prices, delivery and appointments
  • Lead qualification and details captured before handoff
  • Tracking how many chats each ad brings
  • Fewer missed messages and waiting customers

Who is it for?

  • Shops and stores selling through messages
  • Restaurants taking orders on WhatsApp
  • Clinics and centers booking appointments
  • Any business getting more messages than it can answer

How we work

  1. 01

    Review how you take orders today and the common questions

  2. 02

    Set up auto-replies and the chat flow

  3. 03

    Launch ads that open WhatsApp directly

  4. 04

    Track chat volume and cost and improve replies

Questions about this service

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

Yes, the free WhatsApp Business app is enough to start. For advanced automation we use tools like ManyChat or the WhatsApp API.

Does the bot replace human replies?

No, it helps. It answers repetitive questions and organizes details, and you close with customers ready to buy.

How do I know ads brought sales?

We track chats and cost per chat, and log completed orders to calculate realistic ROI.
