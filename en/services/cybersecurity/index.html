Cybersecurity

A hacked ad account or a website that's down for a single day can cost more than a year of protection. I help you find the weak points in your digital business before someone else does, and secure your website, accounts and customer data with clear, practical steps.

What you get

  • Security review of your website or app to uncover common vulnerabilities
  • Protection for social media and Business Manager accounts with two-factor authentication and the right permissions
  • SSL certificate, automatic backups and updates set up
  • Review of staff and agency access to your accounts
  • Training your team to spot phishing and scam messages
  • A clear risk report and a fix plan ordered by priority

Who is it for?

  • Store and website owners handling customer data or payments
  • Businesses that rely on Instagram and Facebook accounts for sales
  • Businesses that faced a hacking attempt and want to secure themselves
  • Teams working on shared accounts that need organized permissions

How we work

  1. 01

    Identify the systems and accounts you own and agree on the scope in writing

  2. 02

    Review settings, permissions and weak points

  3. 03

    Deliver a risk report ordered by priority

  4. 04

    Apply the fixes and prepare a monitoring and rapid-response plan

Questions about this service

Do you hack accounts or recover someone else's account?

No. We only work on protecting systems and accounts you own or are authorized to manage, with clear permission. Any request to break into other people's accounts is refused.

My account was hacked. What should I do?

Change your password right away if you can, turn on two-factor authentication, and follow the platform's official recovery steps. We can guide you through them and secure the account once it's back.

Is protection ever 100%?

No system is 100% secure. The goal is to reduce risk significantly, detect problems quickly, and have a clear plan for handling any incident.
