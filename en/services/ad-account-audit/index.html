Ad Account Audit

If you're spending on ads and results are unclear, the problem is usually in a specific place: missing tracking, wrong structure, or ads that don't convince. The audit pinpoints it and gives you an improvement plan, whether you apply it yourself or we work on it together.

Book a consultation Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Full review of your Meta, TikTok or Snapchat accounts
  • Pixel, tracking and events check
  • Campaign structure and budget assessment
  • Ads and audiences analysis: what works and what doesn't
  • A report of the biggest mistakes wasting your budget
  • A clear improvement plan and a WhatsApp walkthrough

Who is it for?

  • Anyone spending on ads with weak results
  • DIY advertisers wanting an expert opinion
  • Businesses with an agency wanting to verify the work
  • Before deciding on a monthly plan with me

How we work

  1. 01

    You grant view access to your accounts

  2. 02

    I review accounts, tracking and campaigns

  3. 03

    I prepare a report with mistakes and an improvement plan

  4. 04

    We do a 30-minute WhatsApp walkthrough

Questions about this service

Do you need my password?

Never. You add me through Business Manager and can remove access anytime.

How long does the audit take?

Usually a few days depending on account size; I'll confirm the exact timing after seeing them.

What if I don't have an ad account yet?

The audit is for existing accounts. If you haven't started, the free consultation fits you better.
