FAQ

Common questions before we start

What's the minimum ad budget?

I recommend at least $300/month so there's enough data to test and optimize. We'll set the right number together after the consultation.

When will I see results?

The first two weeks are testing and the algorithm's learning phase. Results usually stabilize and improve from week three onward.

Does the ad account stay in my name?

Yes. I work on your ad account and pages through Business Manager access, and all data stays yours.

Do you make the designs and videos?

I write concepts, scripts and ad copy and direct the shoot. Design and editing are included in Scale or available as an add-on.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month-to-month, and you can stop anytime with 15 days' notice.
