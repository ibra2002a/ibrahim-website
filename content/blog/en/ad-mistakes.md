When I review a new ad account, the same mistakes show up almost every time, whatever the business. The good news is that most of them are easy to fix once you know them.

## 1. Advertising without tracking

Launching a campaign without the Pixel, Conversions API and clear events means you can't tell which ad brought the sales. **Fix:** set up tracking before spending any budget, and confirm that purchase or sign-up events actually arrive.

## 2. Choosing the wrong campaign objective

An engagement campaign brings people who engage, and a traffic campaign brings people who click. **Fix:** choose the objective closest to the result you want, such as sales, leads or messages.

## 3. Relying on the "Boost post" button

Boosting is quick, but it gives you limited control over the objective, the audience and the measurement. **Fix:** use Ads Manager for campaigns meant to sell.

## 4. Constant editing

Changing the budget, audience and ads every day stops the campaign from stabilizing and learning. **Fix:** set a clear testing plan and leave enough time before each decision.

## 5. The same ad for months

Even the best ad gets tired. When frequency rises and costs climb, the audience is bored of the same message. **Fix:** prepare new ad ideas regularly and test them before the current ad weakens.

## 6. Judging ads by likes

An ad with lots of likes may not sell, and a quiet one may be the most profitable. **Fix:** judge by cost per result and return, not by engagement.

## 7. Ignoring what happens after the click

A slow page, an unclear offer or a late WhatsApp reply wastes customers you already paid to reach. **Fix:** review the whole customer journey, from the ad to the completed order.

## The takeaway

Before increasing the budget, make sure the foundation is solid: correct tracking, the right objective, organized testing and fast follow-up. Scaling an account with these mistakes multiplies the losses, not the results.
